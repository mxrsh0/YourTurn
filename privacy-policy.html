Privacy & Policy

This notice explains how YourTurn intends to collect, use, protect and retain personal information when providing its UK-focused job discovery and application services.

Important:

This is a working privacy notice for the website and must be reviewed and completed against the final technical setup, legal entity, data processors, retention periods and registration requirements before YourTurn launches or begins processing substantial personal data. It is not a substitute for independent legal advice.

1. Who we are

YourTurn is a UK-focused job discovery service. For privacy enquiries and data protection requests, contact support@yourturn.org.uk. The final legal entity name and registered/contact address will be stated here once confirmed.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, this may include account details, contact details, profile and CV information, saved jobs, applications, application status, messages, support correspondence, partner information, technical information and security logs.

We aim to collect only information that is adequate, relevant and necessary for the purpose for which it is used.

3. How we use information

  • To create and operate user accounts.
  • To provide job search, saving, application and application-status features.
  • To send service messages and important application updates.
  • To communicate with employers or recruitment partners where an applicant chooses to apply or where the service requires information to be passed to the relevant recipient.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, malicious activity and attempts to misuse the service.
  • To manage partner accounts, job postings, analytics and compliance with YourTurn partner rules.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations where required.

4. Lawful bases

Where UK GDPR applies, the lawful basis will depend on the processing. Depending on the feature, this may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent, or compliance with a legal obligation. We will not rely on consent where another lawful basis is more appropriate.

5. Job listings and third-party websites

YourTurn may collect and display job information from employers, recruitment agencies, public sources and other job services. Job listings may link to or otherwise involve third-party websites. Those third parties have their own privacy notices and terms. YourTurn does not control their processing and you should review their information before submitting personal data directly to them.

6. Applications

Where an application is made through a third-party employer or recruitment service, that recipient may become responsible for the personal data you submit to it. Where YourTurn directly facilitates an application, we will explain the relevant data sharing and use before submission where required.

7. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with relevant employers or recruitment partners when necessary to provide an application service, with service providers that help us operate the website under appropriate contractual arrangements, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

8. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Retention

Personal information will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, taking account of legal, regulatory, security, dispute and operational requirements. Specific retention periods will be documented as the platform's systems are finalised.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

YourTurn may use necessary technologies to operate the service and, where applicable, analytics or preference technologies. Non-essential technologies will be handled in accordance with applicable UK requirements, including any consent requirements.

11. Your rights

You may have rights to be informed, access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object, request portability and exercise rights relating to certain automated decision-making. See our GDPR Rights page for a plain-language explanation.

12. Children

YourTurn is a UK job service intended for people who are legally able to seek the relevant employment opportunities. We do not knowingly seek unnecessary personal information from children.

13. Changes

We may update this notice when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page.

14. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to resolve a data protection concern. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office where you believe your data protection rights have not been respected.
