PRIVACY SETUP

You decide what employers see.

These controls apply when you apply to employers through YourTurn. They do not change what YourTurn needs to securely hold in your private account.

NameShow your name to an employer.
AgeShow your age rather than your date of birth.
EthnicityAllow this information to be shared with an employer.
GenderAllow this information to be shared with an employer.
LocationShow your general location to help with suitability.
Right to workShow your stated right-to-work position.
UK residence informationShare your stated UK residence history/evidence status.
Date of birthShare your full date of birth. Usually best kept hidden.
Profile pictureShow your chosen profile picture with applications.
Your privacy choices can be changed later from your account settings. Turning an item off means YourTurn should not include it in employer-facing application data. Some information may still need to be processed privately to operate the service or meet legal obligations.
