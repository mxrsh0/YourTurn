OUR EXPECTATION

Partners are expected to care about the people applying.

YourTurn is designed to save job seekers time. Employers and agencies using our tools must provide accurate information, avoid misleading or scam-style opportunities, give applicants a realistic response timeframe, and update applicants when a decision is made. If an employer cannot respond within its stated timeframe, it should keep applicants informed and provide a genuine reason for the delay.

We may pause or remove listings or partner access where these standards are repeatedly ignored.
