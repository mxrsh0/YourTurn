A QUICK NOTE ON QUALIFICATIONS

Not every online certificate is a formal qualification.

Before putting a course on your CV, check what the provider actually awards. OpenLearn, for example, clearly distinguishes its Statements of Participation and digital badges from formal qualifications. For GCSEs and recognised qualifications, use official or regulated routes and check eligibility before enrolling.
