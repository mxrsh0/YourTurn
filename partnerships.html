Partner with YourTurn

Partnerships give employers and recruitment agencies access to tools designed to make job posting, applicant management and recruitment analytics simpler.

What partners receive

  • Job posting and management tools.
  • Applicant and application-management tools.
  • Recruitment and listing analytics.
  • A dedicated partner area for managing YourTurn activity.

Cost

Partnership access costs £10 per year after the introductory period. Your first year is free and no payment details are required for the free first year.

How to become a partner

Email support@yourturn.org.uk and tell us that you are interested in partnering with YourTurn. We will provide the next steps.

Existing non-partner listings

If a job is posted or featured on YourTurn through our own posting activity and the employer or poster is not yet a formal partner, that employer may receive free access to relevant analytics and partner tools for that listing. This access does not automatically create a paid partnership.

Our standards for partners

YourTurn is designed around a simple principle: applicants deserve to be treated like people, not numbers. Partners are expected to maintain accurate listings, provide realistic employment information and communicate with applicants in a timely and professional manner.

  • Applicants should receive an outcome or meaningful update rather than being left indefinitely without communication.
  • Where practical, employers should respond to applications within 10 days.
  • Where a response cannot be provided within that period, a genuine and reasonable explanation should be available.
  • Rejected applicants should be informed rather than silently ignored.
  • Listings must not be misleading, fraudulent, exploitative or designed to obtain deposits or payments from job seekers.
  • Commission-only or speculative earning opportunities are generally not accepted unless they meet YourTurn's published quality requirements.
  • Employers must cooperate with reasonable YourTurn investigations into applicant complaints and listing quality.

Enforcement

YourTurn may pause, remove or decline listings, restrict partner access, or end a partnership where an account repeatedly breaches these standards, misleads applicants, behaves abusively, or otherwise undermines the safety and quality of the platform. We may request evidence or clarification before taking action and will use reasonable judgement based on the circumstances.
